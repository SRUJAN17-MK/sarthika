Biography

Early Life

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a modest Tamil Muslim family, young Kalam helped his father sell newspapers and made wooden bangles to supplement the family income. Despite financial hardships, his parents instilled in him strong values of honesty, hard work, and faith. His childhood experiences of poverty shaped his determination to succeed and his deep empathy for the common people of India.

Education

Kalam pursued his education with unwavering dedication, studying at Schwartz Higher Secondary School in Ramanathapuram and later earning a degree in physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, affiliated to the University of Madras. He then studied aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT). His passion for flight and space exploration was ignited during these formative years, setting the foundation for his remarkable career in aerospace science and defense technology.

Scientific Career

Dr. Kalam joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) in 1958, where he began his journey in aerospace engineering. He later moved to the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) as the project director of India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III). His work on the Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV) and the development of India's guided missiles earned him the title "Missile Man." He played a pivotal role in developing India's nuclear capabilities and space program.

Presidency & Legacy

Serving as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007, Dr. Kalam was affectionately known as the "People's President." After his presidency, he dedicated his life to education and youth development, visiting schools and universities across India. He authored several inspirational books including "Wings of Fire" and "Ignited Minds." Dr. Kalam passed away on July 27, 2015, while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong, living up to his belief that one should work until their last breath.

Timeline

1931

Birth

Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a Tamil Muslim family.

1950

Higher Education

Graduated in Physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, and joined Madras Institute of Technology for aerospace engineering.

1958

DRDO Career Begins

Joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) as a scientist, starting his distinguished career in defense research.

1969

ISRO & SLV Project

Transferred to ISRO as project director for India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III), which successfully launched in 1980.

1980

SLV-III Success

India's indigenous satellite launch vehicle placed the Rohini satellite in orbit, marking a historic achievement in Indian space program.

1980s

Missile Development

Led the development of Agni and Prithvi missiles under the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP).

1992

DRDO Chief

Appointed as Chief Scientific Adviser to the Minister of Defence and Secretary of the Department of Defence Research and Development.

1997

Bharat Ratna

Conferred India's highest civilian honor, the Bharat Ratna, along with Professor C.N.R. Rao and engineer Verghese Kurien.

2002

President of India

Elected as the 11th President of India, serving as the "People's President" until 2007.

2015

Final Journey

Passed away on July 27, 2015, in Shillong while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong, leaving behind an immortal legacy.
